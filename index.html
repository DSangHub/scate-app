Private Chats That Respect Everyone

Chats Privados que Respetan a Todos

Secure 1:1 conversations between residents and candidates (or their assistants). Free and unlimited for people. Time-capped or unlimited for candidates based on plan.

Conversaciones 1:1 seguras entre residentes y candidatos (o sus asistentes). Gratis e ilimitado para las personas. Con límite de tiempo o ilimitado para candidatos según el plan.

Powered by Stream Chat

Real-time private messaging with read receipts, typing indicators, file sharing, and moderation tools. Residents initiate freely; candidates stay within their plan limits.

Roles & Assistants

Clear roles: Resident (always free), Candidate ($10/30 min), Winner/Loser ($25 unlimited). Assistants can reply on behalf of a candidate without sharing the main account.

Usage Tracking Built-In

Server-side tracking of chat time or messages for the $10 plan. Automatic enforcement so the 30-minute monthly limit is respected while $25 plans remain unrestricted.
